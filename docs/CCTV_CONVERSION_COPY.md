## Camera package comparison

| What matters     | 2MP Analog HD                                            | 4MP IP                                                               |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Image detail     | Full HD (1920 × 1080) for everyday monitoring            | Higher resolution for more detail when reviewing footage             |
| Recorder         | Compatible HD DVR                                        | Compatible NVR                                                       |
| Wiring and power | Typically coaxial video cable plus camera power          | Ethernet; one cable for data and power with compatible PoE equipment |
| Upgrade planning | Existing coaxial wiring may be reusable after inspection | Plan network cabling, PoE capacity, and recorder compatibility       |
| Mobile viewing   | Compatible recorder and internet required                | Compatible system and internet required                              |
| Package pricing  | HD DVR packages start at ₹13,900 for 2 cameras           | IP upgrade quoted after survey; compare the same camera count        |

Existing prices come from the website’s package cards. The comparison introduces no new IP price. Exact models, camera counts, storage, cable allowances, taxes, and inclusions must be confirmed in the quotation. Resolution alone does not establish night clarity, recognition distance, or recording duration.
