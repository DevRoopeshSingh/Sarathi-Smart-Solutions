Frequently Asked Questions

          

          

            Clear answers to help you choose the right smart technology setup for your premises.
          

        


        

          

            
              Can I view my CCTV cameras on my phone when I am away from home?
              
            
            

              

                Yes. Every CCTV installation we configure includes secure mobile application setup.
                As long as your premises has an active broadband or Wi-Fi connection, you can watch
                live HD video, review past recordings, and receive instant motion alerts anywhere in
                the world.
              

            

          


          

            
              How do smart locks, doorbells, and CCTV function during power cuts?
              
            
            

              

                Smart locks run on internal long-life batteries (with low-battery alerts and
                physical backup keys/emergency USB power), so they remain 100% active during power
                failures. For CCTV and Wi-Fi, we provide router and NVR UPS backup solutions to keep
                your system recording without interruption.
              

            

          


          

            
              Do you provide complete wiring, cabling, and clean concealment?
              
            
            

              

                Yes. We pride ourselves on clean, aesthetic workmanship. We handle structured CAT6
                cabling, protective PVC conduit casing, patch panels, and discreet camera placement
                so your home or office remains clutter-free without messy visible wires.
              

            

          


          

            
              How does the site inspection and quotation process work?
              
            
            

              

                Use our online planner above to assemble your starting requirement, then send it to
                us via WhatsApp or phone. We will arrange a free on-site survey across
                Mira–Bhayandar, evaluate blind spots, Wi-Fi coverage, and cable routes, and provide
                an exact, itemized quotation.
              

            

          


          

            
              What warranty and after-sales support do you provide?
              
            
            

              

                All hardware comes with official 1- to 2-year manufacturer warranties. In addition,
                Sarathi Smart Solutions provides our own installation service warranty and dedicated
                local support for maintenance, system upgrades, and mobile app assistance.
