Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service standards, pricing structure, warranties, and local support in Mira-Bhayandar.

What is the difference between a Wi-Fi range extender and a Mesh system?

Range extenders repeat a degraded signal, cut bandwidth by 50%, and create separate confusing network names. A Mesh system uses intelligent interconnected nodes that broadcast a single unified network with full gigabit speeds and seamless handoff.

Can you run Ethernet cables without damaging my interior decor?

Yes. We use slim, color-matched surface PVC electrical casing routed neatly along skirting boards, door frames, and cornices, or pull cables through existing concealed conduits without wall chipping.

Which Wi-Fi router and networking brands do you install?

We supply, configure, and support industry leaders including TP-Link (Deco & Omada), Ubiquiti UniFi, D-Link, and Netgear, selecting models suited to your property layout and ISP bandwidth.

Can you optimize networking for work-from-home video calls and gaming?

Yes. We set up Quality of Service (QoS) bandwidth priority and dedicated hardwired Cat6 Ethernet drops for work laptops, gaming PCs, and smart TVs to eliminate lag and buffering.
