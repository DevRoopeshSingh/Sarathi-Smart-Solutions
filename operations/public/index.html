What matters 2MP Analog HD 4MP IP
Image detail Full HD (1920 × 1080) for everyday monitoring Higher resolution for more detail when reviewing footage
Recorder Compatible HD DVR Compatible NVR
Wiring & power Typically coaxial video cable plus camera power Ethernet cable; one cable for data and power with compatible PoE equipment
Upgrade planning Existing coaxial wiring may be reusable after inspection Plan network cabling, PoE capacity, and recorder compatibility
Mobile viewing Available with a compatible recorder and internet connection Available with a compatible system and internet connection

                Internet is required for viewing cameras remotely from outside the property. Local
                recording can continue without internet if the recorder and power supply are

                confirming reuse.
