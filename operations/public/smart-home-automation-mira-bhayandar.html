Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service standards, pricing structure, warranties, and local support in Mira-Bhayandar.

Can a digital smart lock be installed on my existing wooden door or safety grill?

Yes. Most digital mortise locks install cleanly on standard wooden doors (minimum 35mm thickness). We also supply specialized slim-backset smart locks designed specifically for metal safety grill doors.

What happens if the smart lock battery drains completely?

Smart locks provide low-battery voice alerts weeks in advance. If batteries drain completely, every lock includes emergency manual keys and an external USB port for instant power-bank jump-starting.

Do I need to redo my house wiring to install smart automation?

No rewiring or wall chipping is needed. Our retrofit smart switch modules sit directly behind your current switchboard plates, leaving your existing switches, tiles, and interior paint untouched.

Can I control my smart devices while travelling outside India?

Yes. As long as your home Wi-Fi is online and your smartphone has an active internet connection, you can monitor door locks, operate lights, and receive security notifications worldwide.
