Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Sarathi Smart Solutions, Bhayander East

Security systems, network switches, and biometric terminals operate 24/7 in demanding environments. Our Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) packages provide preventive health checks, priority response, and optical cleaning to ensure continuous uptime for residential societies, offices, and commercial establishments in Mira-Bhayandar and Mumbai MMR.

1. Types of AMC Contracts

  • Non-Comprehensive AMC (Standard): Covers routine preventive visits, labor, alignment, lens cleaning, system diagnostics, and emergency breakdown visits. Replacement parts or damaged cables are billed separately at transparent trade rates.
  • Comprehensive AMC (By Custom Agreement): Covers labor, scheduled maintenance, and replacement of specified wearing components as outlined in the dedicated agreement document.

2. Routine Inclusions

Every standard CCTV / Security AMC visit includes:

  • Quarterly scheduled on-site preventive inspections.
  • Camera lens cleaning, moisture check, and angle adjustments.
  • Hard disk health check, recording integrity verification, and playback tests.
  • Power supply (SMPS) voltage check and connector tightness inspection.
  • Mobile remote viewing connectivity and firmware status check.
  • Priority response for breakdown calls (typically within 24 to 48 business hours in Mira-Bhayandar).

3. Exclusions

Unless explicitly covered under a customized comprehensive agreement, AMC services do not include:

  • Cost of new replacement cameras, recorders, or hard drives damaged by lightning or external electrical surges.
  • Major rewiring or new cable runs necessitated by building renovation or rat bites.
  • Active internet broadband subscription fees or ISP-side troubleshooting.
