Fast Diagnosis for Offline Cameras & Video Loss

We identify whether video faults stem from faulty SMPS power supplies, corroded BNC/DC connectors, severed cables, or camera sensor degradation. We test each line individually to avoid replacing working hardware unnecessarily.

  • Offline camera channels, night-vision IR failure, and flickering video
  • Burnt power supply units (SMPS) and blown DC adapters
  • Intermittent video signals caused by loose or oxidised connections

Hard Drive & Video Playback Fault Rectification

One of the most dangerous CCTV failures is a recorder that displays live video but fails to store footage. We test hard drive sectors, DVR motherboard ports, and firmware settings to ensure continuous 24/7 video recording without gaps.

  • Beeping DVR alerts indicating hard disk read/write failures
  • Playback timestamp freezing and corrupted recording segments
  • Firmware updates and forgotten admin password recovery

Why did my CCTV mobile app stop connecting?

Mobile viewing commonly disconnects when the broadband router restarts with a new IP address, LAN cables become loose, or recorder P2P cloud services need re-binding. We reconfigure mobile access quickly.

Do you repair CCTV systems installed by other vendors?

Yes. We diagnose and service CCTV systems from all major brands (Hikvision, CP Plus, Dahua, UNV) regardless of who originally installed them.

What is the difference between comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC?

A standard non-comprehensive AMC covers regular preventive service visits, labor, and diagnostic inspections. Any hardware replacements (like a new power supply or hard disk) are billed at item rates.
