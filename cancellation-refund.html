Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Sarathi Smart Solutions, Mira-Bhayandar

At Sarathi Smart Solutions, we value complete transparency and fair dealings. This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the terms governing appointment rescheduling, project cancellations, advance deposit refunds, and hardware returns.

1. Site Survey Appointments

  • Free Rescheduling & Cancellation: Initial site survey consultations are completely free of charge. You may reschedule or cancel your survey appointment at any time prior to the technician's arrival without any fee or penalty.
  • Notice: We kindly request at least 2 hours advance notice via phone or WhatsApp at +91 83697 04457 so we can adjust technician schedules across Mira-Bhayandar.

2. Advance Deposit & Order Cancellation

  • Cancellation Before Hardware Procurement: If an approved project is cancelled before hardware items (DVR/NVR, cameras, hard drives, cables) have been dispatched or specifically procured from distributors, 100% of the advance deposit will be refunded.
  • Cancellation After Hardware Procurement: If specialized or custom-ordered hardware has already been mobilized from official distributors, any distributor restocking fees or non-returnable component costs will be deducted from the advance, and the remaining balance will be promptly refunded. Unopened hardware may alternatively be handed over to the client.
  • Cancellation After Installation Commences: Once physical on-site work (cabling, conduit mounting, structural drilling) has started, advance payments will be prorated against the actual materials installed and labor hours completed.

3. Hardware Returns & DOA (Dead on Arrival) Replacements

  • Unopened Hardware: Unopened, uninstalled hardware in its original manufacturer packaging with all serial numbers intact can be returned within 7 calendar days of delivery, subject to manufacturer return eligibility.
  • Defective on Arrival: Any hardware component found defective upon initial unboxing or power-up will be replaced immediately with brand-new equipment under the manufacturer’s Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) replacement window at zero additional cost to you.
  • Consumables: Cut cables, custom conduits, casing, and drilled mounting brackets cannot be returned once physically installed on the premises.

4. Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

  • Cooling-off Period: Annual Maintenance Contracts can be cancelled within 14 days of signing if no emergency breakdown visits or scheduled servicing visits have taken place, for a full refund minus a nominal administrative setup fee.
  • Mid-term AMC Cancellation: Cancellations after service visits have commenced are prorated based on elapsed quarters and services performed, as stipulated in your specific AMC contract schedule.

5. Refund Process & Timelines

  • Method of Refund: Approved refunds are credited directly to the original bank account, UPI ID, or credit/debit instrument used for the payment. Cash refunds are not issued for audit and accounting compliance.
  • Processing Time: Once a refund request is accepted and bank details are confirmed, funds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

6. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund
