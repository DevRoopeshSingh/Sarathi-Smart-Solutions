Warranty Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Sarathi Smart Solutions, Bhayander East

At Sarathi Smart Solutions, we believe genuine equipment and quality workmanship are the foundations of long-term reliability. We provide dual protection: manufacturer hardware warranties and our dedicated on-site workmanship warranty.

1. Hardware OEM Warranties

All electronic equipment supplied by us is 100% genuine and backed by the respective original equipment manufacturer (OEM) warranty policies:

  • CCTV Cameras & DVRs/NVRs: Typically covered by a 1- to 2-year official manufacturer warranty against component manufacturing defects (Hikvision, CP Plus, Dahua).
  • Surveillance Hard Drives: Western Digital Purple / Seagate SkyHawk surveillance drives typically carry 2- to 3-year manufacturer warranties.
  • Smart Digital Locks: Godrej, Yale, and Tuya-enabled locksets carry official brand warranties (typically 1 to 3 years depending on model).
  • Networking Hardware: Routers, access points, and switches carry standard manufacturer warranty as per brand terms (TP-Link, Ubiquiti).

2. Our 1-Year Installation Service Warranty

In addition to hardware warranties, Sarathi Smart Solutions provides a 1-Year Workmanship Warranty covering:

  • Camera angle alignment and mount firmness.
  • Connector crimping and junction box integrity.
  • PVC casing stability and cable route fastening.
  • DVR/NVR initial network configuration and mobile viewing re-connection guidance.

3. Warranty Exclusions

The following circumstances are not covered under standard warranty:

  • Physical damage, vandalism, accidental breakage, or drops.
  • Water ingress into non-weatherproof (indoor) equipment.
  • Damage resulting from lightning strikes, electrical power surges, high voltage fluctuations, or fire. (We strongly recommend spike protectors/UPS).
  • Tampering, repair, or rewiring attempted by unauthorized third parties or third-party electricians.
  • Network disruptions caused by ISP changes, forgotten passwords, or customer router resets.
