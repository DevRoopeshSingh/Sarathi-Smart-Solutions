Frequently Asked Questions

How does Sarathi Smart Solutions assist our society committee during planning?

We conduct a complete property walkthrough with committee members, identify critical coverage zones, calculate storage requirements, and provide a formal written quotation ready for committee review.

How many days of video recording can our society maintain?

We size storage based on your requirements. Most housing societies opt for 15 to 30 days of continuous recording using enterprise surveillance drives and smart H.265+ compression.

Yes. We configure multi-user remote access with separate secure credentials so designated committee members can monitor premises simultaneously.
